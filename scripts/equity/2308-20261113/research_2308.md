## Executive Summary

Over the last six months (May–November 2026), Delta Electronics (2308.TW) delivered record operational scale propelled by AI data center power and liquid cooling demand, alongside cyclical stabilization in Industrial Automation (IA) and ongoing headwinds in Electric Vehicle (EV) Mobility. October 2026 consolidated revenue hit an all-time monthly high of NT$74.666B (+14.17% MoM, +30.12% YoY), lifting 10M26 cumulative revenue to NT$614.334B (+36.32% YoY). In Q3 2026, gross margin temporarily dipped to 34.1% (-1.5 ppt QoQ) due to an ~NT$3.9B (~1.7% of revenue) accounting inventory write-down provision expected to reverse starting Q4 2026 as inventory ships, while operating margin held resilient at 16.6% on lower OpEx ratio (17.5%). Management guided Q4 2026 revenue to exceed Q3 and Q4 gross margin to be no worse than Q3, supported by customer price pass-throughs for ~2% YTD material cost inflation across Q4 2026–Q1 2027. Structurally, ±400V DC HVDC power racks entered mass production in Q3 2026 and 800V DC systems began initial shipments ahead of a major 2027 ramp as AI rack power scales from ~140kW (GB300) toward ~250kW (VR200). Full-year 2026 CapEx is projected at ~NT$70B (+50% YoY) across Thailand, Taiwan, China, the U.S., India, and a newly approved Penang, Malaysia manufacturing base.
