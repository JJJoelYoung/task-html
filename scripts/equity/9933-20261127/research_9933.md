# Research: 9933 CTCI Corporation (中鼎工程)

Gathered 2026-11-28 with the `deep-research` workflow: 5 search angles, 18 fetched sources, 84 extracted claims, the top 25 checked by three independent verifiers each (24 confirmed, 1 refuted). The workflow spent most of its verification budget on the same 3Q26 and backlog figures repeated across five sources, so 59 claims were never voted on, including the BKRF receivable and the litigation. The main session then listed all 84 claims by source, checked every number it could against FinMind, and re-read seven unvoted sources itself (uanalyze 5340859762, 2937259763 and 6142759760; pocket.tw; udn 9441481; winvest 99159). ## Facts

- 3Q26 (reported at the 2026-11-16 investor conference): revenue NT$21.854bn, up about 3% YoY and about 6.5% QoQ; gross margin 13.41% vs 9.28% in 3Q25, the fourth straight double-digit quarter (4Q25 12.65%, 1Q26 13.05%, 2Q26 13.67%); parent net income NT$2.073bn (+173% YoY); basic EPS NT$2.17 (vs 0.85). 1-9M26: revenue NT$59.574bn (about -12% YoY), parent net income NT$4.531bn, basic EPS NT$4.86 (vs a loss of 0.23), diluted EPS NT$4.23 (counts the CB's potential shares). Source: 口袋學堂 (pocket.tw), 2026-11-17. <https://www.pocket.tw/school/articles/article-mwkbf2mq/>
- Backlog under construction (在建工程) reached a record NT$498.1bn at end-September and NT$501.1bn at end-October 2026 (broad high-tech about 20%); new contracts signed reached NT$104.4bn by end-September and NT$117.8bn by end-October 2026, with high-tech the largest category at 49% of 1-3Q wins. Source: 優分析 (uanalyze), 2026-11-16. <https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/5562759660>
- Backlog was NT$450.35bn at end-2025 (then a record) and NT$490.3bn at end-June 2026; at mid-2026 the mix was power 29%, water resources and environment 17%, gas 13%, and Taiwan 88%. Source: CTCI 2026-08-12 investor deck (slide 9). <https://www.ctci.com/www/ctci2022/pdf/EN/202608-1_seminar.pdf>
- 2025 new contracts set a record of NT$181.29bn, nearly 70% of it power engineering; backlog was NT$450.35bn at end-2025. Source: 工商時報 (CTEE), 2026-02-23. <https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20260223701229-430503>
- October 2026 revenue was NT$9.023bn, +7.97% MoM and +53.86% YoY; Jan-Oct revenue NT$68.6bn, -6.7% YoY. The company attributes the YTD decline to slow percentage-of-completion recognition while large contracts signed in 2024-2025 are still in design and procurement, and says revenue will rise gradually in the months after October, hoping to return to a more normal level in 2027 (no numeric guidance). 2025 revenue was NT$91.8bn, so November and December would need to average NT$11.6bn to match it. Taipower's Tongxiao Phase 2 turnkey contract (with Mitsubishi Power, awarded September 2025, NT$154.7bn, five combined-cycle units, 2,800MW, completion due 2031) is not in the 2026 new-contract total and was still in design and procurement 14 months after award. Source: 優分析 (uanalyze), 2026-11-16 (the Tongxiao and 2025-revenue details were not voted; re-read). <https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/6142759760>
- October 2026 revenue NT$9.023bn, +7.97% MoM, +53.86% YoY. The company's monthly revenue note says contracts signed in 2025 are gradually reaching peak execution toward the end of 2026. The page lists 3Q26 EPS as 2.15, which is 1-9M26 EPS (4.86) minus 1H26 EPS (2.71); the reported standalone figure is 2.17. Source: Win 投資 (winvest), 2026-11-15 (the note and the EPS line were not voted; re-read). <https://winvest.tw/News/Detail/99159>
- Margin drivers: legacy COVID-era fixed-price contracts with unrecoverable overruns are nearly all recognized (management: 都已經即將告一段落了), and contracts won in the last 2-3 years build in escalation and contingency; after award the company locks copper on the LME and locks FX. Management is cautiously optimistic about holding margins. Analyst consensus for 2027 gross margin is reported at about 12% (single source, low confidence). Source: 優分析 (uanalyze), 2026-11-16. <https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/3175259761>
- At the 2026-08-12 call the company said the difficult pandemic-era contracts were fully recognized, and that a 2Q26 non-operating gain of about NT$0.29bn came from copper hedges (FinMind shows 2Q26 non-operating income of NT$0.290bn); it also said the US renewable diesel plant was running stably. Source: Fugle call memo (AI summary of the call), 2026-08-12. <https://blog.fugle.tw/post/earnings-call-9933-2026-08-12>
- 2026 high-tech wins include a Singapore fab-facilities job for a US memory maker (signed 1Q26; reported as Micron, Woodlands) and a US AI server assembly plant; large power projects (通霄二期, 大林) are moving into construction. Source: 經濟日報 (udn), 2026-05-14. <https://udn.com/news/story/7252/9502921>
- Region mix (re-read): the 12-month bid pipeline is NT$1.219 trillion (Taiwan NT$533.0bn, Middle East NT$456.0bn, Southeast Asia and India NT$200.0bn, US and Mexico NT$27.0bn, China NT$3.0bn), which are bid opportunities, not orders. Jan-Oct 2026 new contracts were Taiwan 63%, Southeast Asia 30%, Middle East 1%; end-September backlog was Taiwan 87%, Middle East 2%; consolidated revenue (period not labelled) was Taiwan 70%, Middle East 13%. The article reads this as Middle East jobs winding down with no new ones signed; CTCI was not on the January 2025 shortlist for the fifth NGL train at Ruwais. Overseas owners want a single general contractor, unlike Taiwan high-tech owners, who split packages; CTCI is GC for a US$1bn Bangalore campus for a Taiwanese electronics maker. Source: 優分析 (uanalyze), 2026-11-16 (not voted; re-read). <https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/2937259763>
- In April 2026 CTCI brought in Delta Electronics (2308), the USI/Taiwan Polymer group (1304) and the Ta Ya group (1609) as strategic investors through a private placement, forming a technology-engineering alliance aimed at AI infrastructure, net zero and Middle East reconstruction. Source: 旺得富 (wantrich, China Times), 2026-05-07. <https://wantrich.chinatimes.com/news/20260507900740-420101>
- Private placement priced 2026-04-14: 45,099 lots (about 45.1M shares) at NT$35.48, about NT$1.6bn, a 2.3% premium to that day's close of 34.65; Delta took 28,184 lots, Taiwan Polymer and Asia Polymer 4,230 each, Ta Ya 8,455; the rest of the 90,000-lot quota approved at the May 2025 AGM lapses. The placement was approved to add working capital and strengthen the balance sheet after about NT$19.604bn of BKRF receivables at subsidiary CTCIA were stuck in the owner's parent's (GCEH) US reorganization. Source: 經濟日報 (udn, CNA wire), 2026-04-14 (not voted; re-read). <https://udn.com/news/story/7241/9441481> Also 旺得富 (wantrich), 2026-04-15. <https://wantrich.chinatimes.com/news/20260415900414-420101> The implied share count rose from about 892M to about 955M in FinMind.
- Second domestic unsecured CB (中鼎二, 99332): NT$6.0bn issued, about NT$5.999bn still outstanding; conversion price NT$43.6 (reset from 53.9, 51.8 and 45.5); in the money at the 2026-11-27 close of 54.1 (parity about 124); full conversion adds about 137.6M shares, about 14% of shares outstanding; next put date 2027-07-23, maturity 2029-07-23. Source: 口袋學堂 (pocket.tw), 2026-11-17, for the conversion price. <https://www.pocket.tw/school/articles/article-mwkbf2mq/> Outstanding balance and dates: thefew CB quote page, as of 2026-11-27. <https://thefew.tw/quote/99332>

- BKRF (Bakersfield renewable-fuel plant, owner GCEH, Chapter 11 filed 2025-04-16, plan effective 2025-08): CTCI announced an unrecovered construction receivable of NT$19.604bn, 15.7% of consolidated total assets at the time, secured under US law; an impairment of about NT$3.157bn was booked in 1Q25 opex. The plant runs at a little over 9,000 bbl/day (design 15,000) and earns more than the reorganization forecast; the reorganization forecast had partial working-capital repayment from end-2028, which management now expects earlier; CTCI and the equity holder plan to sell the plant company around 2030 to recover the receivable; the 45Z tax credit covers only 2025-2027 production. 3Q26 operating profit was NT$2.47bn (FinMind: 2.473bn). Source: 優分析 (uanalyze), 2026-11-16 (not voted; re-read). <https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/5340859762>
- BKRF at end-September 2026 and cash flow (re-read): the gross long-term receivable on the US project was still US$609M, with a loss allowance already booked; recovery depends on the reorganization plan. Expected credit losses were about NT$3.048bn in 1-9M25 and about NT$0.018bn in 1-9M26; adding each back, operating income rose from NT$4.858bn to NT$6.453bn. 1-9M26 operating cash flow was NT$19.516bn (vs an outflow of 0.561bn), with lower receivables adding about NT$10.2bn and higher contract liabilities about NT$7.0bn. Source: 口袋學堂 (pocket.tw), 2026-11-17 (not voted; re-read). <https://www.pocket.tw/school/articles/article-mwkbf2mq/>
- Litigation disclosed in the 3Q26 financial report (re-read): 三元能源 claims at least NT$4.626bn from CTCI and its subsidiary 益鼎 over a fire at a Kaohsiung lithium-battery plant (Taipei District Court); Taipower claims about NT$2.799bn plus about US$540.7k from CTCI and co-contractor GE Vernova over the shutdown of Hsinta Power Plant Unit 1. CTCI says it delivered as contracted in the first case and that it and its partner are not liable for the shutdown in the second; final liability is undetermined. Source: 口袋學堂 (pocket.tw), 2026-11-17. <https://www.pocket.tw/school/articles/article-mwkbf2mq/>
